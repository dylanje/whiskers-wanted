About Us

Whisker Wanted exists because most adoption listings read like DMV paperwork, and cats deserve better marketing. We feature one cat at a time — fully, honestly, red flags and all — so the right person can fall for the whole animal instead of a blurry photo and a checklist of vaccinations.

We're a small volunteer effort, not a shelter ourselves. We work with local rescues and foster networks to spotlight cats who've been waiting the longest or who just need someone to write their bio with a little more personality. Once a cat is adopted, they graduate to the Purr-manent Record, and the next cat in line gets their moment in the spotlight.

Our mission is simple: fewer cats waiting, more people who know exactly what they're signing up for before they bring a new roommate home.

Made with love (and mild claw damage) for cats everywhere.
