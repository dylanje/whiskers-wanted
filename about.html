About Us

Whiskers Wanted exists because my sister has a cat that needs adopted. Sure, I could just share a picture of the cat to my IG story but where is the fun in that? Instead I decided to make a website that can be fun and informative, and if this goes well we could possibly extend it to other cats.

If you have a cat that needs adopted, please reach out to me and I will see if I can help you make a listing for your cat.

Made with love (and mild claw damage) for cats everywhere.
