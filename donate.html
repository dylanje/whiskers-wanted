Keep the Lights (and Sunbeams) On

Whisker Wanted runs on volunteer time, hosting costs, and the occasional bribe of treats to get a good photo. Donations go straight toward site upkeep, printing materials for our partner rescues, and small grants toward vet visits for cats waiting to be featured.

$10

Covers a week of hosting so the site stays up.

$25

Chips in toward a featured cat's vet check-up.

$50

Helps a partner rescue with food and litter for a month.

Any amount

Every bit helps a cat get closer to their forever couch.

To donate, reach out to us at donate@example.com and we'll send over the details — we're working on adding online payments soon.
